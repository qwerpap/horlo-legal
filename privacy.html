Privacy Policy

Horlo: Intermittent Fasting · Last updated September 28, 2026

Horlo is an intermittent fasting timer and journal. Your fasts, water, weight and mood stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your journal, and we don't sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

  • Fasts you start and end, their plan, length and how you felt.
  • Glasses of water, weight entries and daily mood you log.
  • Your fasting plan, schedule, units and your answers to the first-run questions.

The Home Screen widget and the Live Activity read a small copy of the current fast (start time, goal and plan) from the app's own shared container on your device.

Notifications

If you allow them, Horlo schedules local reminders on your iPhone (fast complete, eating window closing, scheduled start). They are created on the device; nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Horlo receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier, purchase status and your first-run answers in general form (goal, fasting experience, what got in the way) to validate purchases and measure how the paywall performs. It never receives your fasts, weight, water or mood.

Analytics and tracking

Horlo does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above. There are no ads.

Deleting your data

Remove any entry with the ✕ next to it, or delete Horlo from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

Not medical advice

Horlo is a timer and journal, not a medical device. Fasting stages are general estimates from published research, not measurements of your body. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, diabetic, underweight, have a history of eating disorders or take medication, talk to your doctor before fasting.

Children

Horlo is not directed at children under 13.
