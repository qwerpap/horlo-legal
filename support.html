Support

Horlo: Intermittent Fasting · Last updated September 28, 2026

Questions, a bug, or a billing problem? Write to netkaktakto@gmail.com — a real person answers, usually within a day. In the app: Settings › Write to us adds your app version automatically.

Common questions

  • Cancel a subscription: Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions › Horlo › Cancel. Nothing else is needed.
  • Restore Pro on a new iPhone: Settings in Horlo › Restore purchases.
  • The Live Activity doesn't show: iPhone Settings › Horlo › Live Activities must be on.
  • Change the start of a running fast: tap "Started" under the timer.
